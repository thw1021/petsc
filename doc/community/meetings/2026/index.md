The 2026 PETSc annual user meeting will be hosted by the Firedrake Team in Oxford, UK, during the week of June 1~5, 2026.
PETSc occupies the first half of the meeting (June 1-3, 2026), for which you can register, but we encourage everyone to attend the whole week as many amazing things are going on in Firedrake Land.

Please see details here, https://www.firedrakeproject.org/firedrake_26.html
